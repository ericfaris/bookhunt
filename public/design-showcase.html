Direction exploration

Three directions were generated and evaluated against BookHunt's actual purpose (a cozy, personal, task-focused book-hunting tool) before committing to a direction. Full writeup in DESIGN.md §2.

Warm Paper & Ink Hunt✓ chosen
Cream paper, navy ink, hot-orange accent, rounded friendly type. Matches the existing logo and the app's real personality.
Reading Room Noir
Rejected — moody library aesthetic fights the app's task-focused, in-and-out usage and its existing light/dark toggle.
Field Guide Ephemera
Rejected — charming but adds vintage-curio kitsch the app doesn't need; green/red accents would collide with the semantic good/danger tokens.
